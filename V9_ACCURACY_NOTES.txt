SmartGradeScanner OMR Ultra v9

The v9 answer engine is a deterministic on-device visual-evidence fusion system.
It does not use OCR to infer A/B/C/D/E. For built-in sheets, answer choice is derived
from the geometric position of the filled bubble: left-to-right = A, B, C, D, E.

Safety rule: a low-confidence/tied row is flagged for review instead of being assigned
a confident answer. No OMR system can honestly guarantee zero errors on arbitrary
photos, handwriting, folds, glare or damaged sheets; v9 is designed to prefer review
over a confident wrong grade.

See TestAssets/V9-VISUAL-FUSION-VALIDATION.txt for deterministic degradation tests.
